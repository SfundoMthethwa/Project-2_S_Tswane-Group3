About Campus Gig

Grow Smart. Grow Within.

Campus Gig is a web application designed to connect students with flexible part-time work opportunities while helping employers reach talented students.

Find a Job
Students can discover flexible, student-friendly job opportunities that can fit around their studies.
Track Applications
Students can keep track of the jobs they have applied for and manage their application progress.
Post a Job
Employers can post opportunities and connect with students who are available and ready to work.
Career Growth
Students can gain practical experience, develop workplace skills and build experience while studying.
Simple and Protected Access
Features that require an account ask users to identify themselves as a student or employer before continuing.
